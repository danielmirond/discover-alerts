COSRX is probably the best-selling Korean skincare brand outside Asia. Its Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence has over 100,000 reviews on Amazon. Its Low pH Good Morning Cleanser is the most recommended cleanser on r/AsianBeauty. And its BHA Blackhead Power Liquid is the go-to chemical exfoliant for oily skin.

But with success came a serious problem: counterfeits. In 2024, COSRX launched its verification system [COSRX Record](https://official.cosrx.com/record) to combat the wave of fake products flooding Amazon, eBay and unauthorized shops. If you use COSRX and don't verify, you could be applying a product without the ingredients you expect. or worse, with ingredients that damage your skin.

## The counterfeit problem

### Why COSRX is the most counterfeited brand

Three reasons: accessible pricing ($12-22), massive demand (millions of units per month) and minimalist packaging that's easy to imitate. An $18 product with 100 million TikTok views is the perfect target for counterfeiters.

### What's in a fake COSRX

Analysis of counterfeit products has found:
- **Water with thickener** instead of 96% snail filtrate
- **Undeclared fragrances** that cause contact dermatitis
- **Preservatives banned in the EU** (formaldehyde, methylisothiazolinone)
- **Incorrect pH** in cleansers (pH 8-9 instead of 5.5)
- **No real BHA** in the Blackhead Power Liquid

A fake product doesn't just not work: it can destroy your skin barrier.

### How to verify authenticity: COSRX Record

COSRX implemented a verification system at [official.cosrx.com/record](https://official.cosrx.com/record):

1. **Find the QR code** on the product packaging
2. **Scan it with your phone** or enter the code manually
3. **COSRX Record confirms** if the product is authentic
4. If there's no QR code or the system doesn't recognize it: **it's fake or from a batch before the system launched**

### Signs of a fake product

| Sign | Authentic | Fake |
|---|---|---|
| **QR code** | Present, verifiable at cosrx.com/record | Absent or unverifiable |
| **Snail 96 texture** | Viscous, transparent, no smell | Watery, cloudy, or has odor |
| **Cleanser pH** | 5.0-6.0 (test with strip) | 7.0-9.0 |
| **Packaging** | Crisp letters, readable barcode | Blurry letters, smudged ink |
| **Seller** | Authorized (Amazon official, YesStyle, Olive Young) | Third-party seller without verification |
| **Price** | $12-22 depending on product | Too cheap to be real |

## Where to buy authentic COSRX

### Authorized sellers

- **Amazon** (seller "COSRX Official" or "Jolse"). verify "sold by" is an authorized Korean shop
- **YesStyle**. authorized retailer, ships from Asia (7-14 days)
- **Olive Young Global**. the reference K-Beauty store in Korea, international shipping
- **Soko Glam**. US-based K-Beauty retailer

### Risky sellers

- Third-party Amazon sellers with no history
- eBay (high counterfeit rate)
- AliExpress (no authenticity guarantee)
- Non-specialized physical stores

### The price rule

If a COSRX Snail 96 (100ml) costs less than $14, it's probably fake. Retail price in the US is $18-24. Legitimate deals go down to $15-16 at most.
