Foreo is a Swedish beauty tech company founded in Stockholm in 2013. In just over a decade, it has become one of the most recognized beauty device brands in the world, with a presence in more than 80 countries. Their approach: applying advanced technology to personal care routines, with eye-catching designs and medical-grade silicone materials.

In 2026, the Foreo catalog spans four main product lines, each targeting a different need. This guide explains what each device does, who it is designed for, what the science says, and above all, when the Swedish brand's premium price is worth paying and when more reasonable alternatives exist.

## The four Foreo product lines in 2026

Foreo has built its ecosystem around four distinct technologies. This is not an "all-in-one" device: each unit does one thing and does it well. This has advantages (specialization, dedicated power) and disadvantages (total cost if you want more than one).

### 1. Luna -- Sonic facial cleansing

The Luna line is where Foreo started and remains its flagship product. It uses T-Sonic pulsations (transdermal sonic pulsations) transmitted through medical-grade silicone touchpoints to dislodge dirt, sebum, and makeup residue from pores.

The Luna 4 Plus is the current most complete version: 16 pulsation intensities, differentiated zones for sensitive skin and the T-zone, and connectivity with the Foreo app to customize routines. Unlike nylon bristle brushes (like the now-discontinued Clarisonic), Foreo's silicone is non-porous, meaning it does not harbor bacteria and is more hygienic.

The evidence for sonic cleansing is moderate. Several dermatological studies have shown that device-assisted cleansing removes more residue than manual cleansing, especially in the T-zone and around the nose. However, for sensitive or barrier-compromised skin, gentle manual cleansing may be preferable.

Read our [full review of the Foreo Luna 4 Plus](/en/hara/k-beauty/foreo-luna-4-review) for a detailed analysis.

### 2. Bear -- Microcurrent for facial toning

The Bear line applies microcurrents to the facial muscles to stimulate ATP (adenosine triphosphate) production, the cellular energy molecule. The principle: stimulated facial muscles maintain their tone better, combating progressive sagging.

The Bear 2 incorporates five microcurrent levels, simultaneous T-Sonic pulsations, and a system that Foreo calls "Real-Time Skin Coach," which adjusts intensity based on the zone and your skin's conductivity. It is a more sophisticated device than the first generation and competes directly with the NuFace Trinity+.

The evidence for facial microcurrents is moderate. The studies by Cheng et al. (1982) established that microcurrents stimulate ATP production by up to 500% at the cellular level. More recent studies in aesthetic dermatology show improvements in firmness and facial contour after 6-8 weeks of consistent use, although the effects are subtle and require consistency.

Read our [full review of the Foreo Bear 2](/en/hara/k-beauty/foreo-bear-2-review) for results, protocol, and comparisons.

### 3. FAQ 202 -- LED mask with 4 wavelengths

The FAQ 202 is Foreo's most recent and viral device. It is a full-face LED mask that combines four wavelengths: red (630-660nm) for collagen stimulation, blue (415nm) against acne bacteria, orange (590nm) for radiance, and NIR (near-infrared, 830nm) for deep tissue penetration.

Unlike older LED masks that offered one or two wavelengths, the FAQ 202 allows you to combine programs based on your needs. 9-minute sessions, FDA-cleared, and with a facial fit that Foreo has significantly improved compared to the first FAQ version.

The evidence for LED phototherapy is moderate-strong. The study by Wunsch & Matuschka (2014) demonstrated that red LED irradiation at 611-650nm increases collagen density and reduces periorbital wrinkles. Papageorgiou et al. (2000) established the efficacy of blue LED at 415nm against *Propionibacterium acnes*. This is the Foreo device category with the strongest scientific backing.

Read our [full review of the Foreo FAQ 202](/en/hara/k-beauty/foreo-faq-202-review) for the science, comparison with CurrentBody, and the verdict.

### 4. Peach 2 -- At-home IPL hair removal

The Peach line is Foreo's entry into at-home permanent hair removal. It uses IPL (Intense Pulsed Light) to progressively destroy the hair follicle through light absorption by hair melanin. The Peach 2 version incorporates a cooling system that reduces discomfort during treatment.

Compatible with Fitzpatrick skin tones I-V (excludes very dark skin), five intensity levels, and a progressive 8-week treatment protocol. It is the direct competitor to the Braun Silk Expert Pro and Philips Lumea Advanced.

The evidence for at-home IPL is moderate-strong. Multiple dermatological studies have demonstrated that at-home IPL devices achieve 60-80% hair reduction after 6-12 sessions, although results vary significantly depending on hair-skin contrast. It does not work on blonde, red, or gray hair (insufficient melanin).

Read our [full review of the Foreo Peach 2](/en/hara/k-beauty/foreo-peach-2-review) for the protocol, skin type compatibility, and comparisons.

## Who should buy each device

### Luna 4 Plus: for you if...

- You wear makeup daily and want a deeper cleanse than manual washing provides
- You have combination or oily skin with a tendency toward clogged pores
- You already have a skincare routine and want to improve the cleansing step
- You don't mind the upfront investment because there are no consumables (the device lasts for years)

**Not for you if:** you have very sensitive skin, active rosacea, or dermatitis (gentle manual cleansing is preferable), or if you don't wear makeup/sunscreen daily (manual cleansing is sufficient).

### Bear 2: for you if...

- You notice loss of firmness in the jawline or cheeks (typically starting around 35-40)
- You are looking for a subtle, gradual "lifting" effect without invasive procedures
- You have the discipline to use the device 3-5 times per week for months
- You already know the [Medicube AGE-R Booster Pro](/en/hara/k-beauty/medicube-age-r-booster-pro-review) but want something more powerful in microcurrents

**Not for you if:** you expect immediate or dramatic results (microcurrents are cumulative and subtle), you have facial metal implants, you are pregnant, or your budget is limited (the [Medicube](/en/hara/k-beauty/medicube-age-r-booster-pro-review) offers microcurrent + electroporation + LED at $159).

### FAQ 202: for you if...

- You want to stimulate collagen non-invasively with solid scientific evidence
- You have mild-to-moderate acne and are looking for a complementary treatment to your topical actives
- You are interested in phototherapy but don't want to invest in a full red light panel (see our [red light guide](/en/protocols/morning-red-light-protocol))
- You value the convenience of a hands-free 9-minute treatment

**Not for you if:** you have photosensitive conditions, you take photosensitizing medication (isotretinoin, tetracyclines), or you are looking for clinical-level results (professional LED devices have more power per cm2).

### Peach 2: for you if...

- You want to permanently reduce body hair at home
- Your hair is dark (brown to black) and your skin is light to medium (Fitzpatrick I-IV gives the best results)
- You are willing to commit to an 8-week protocol with weekly sessions
- You see at-home hair removal as a more economical long-term alternative to salon treatments

**Not for you if:** your hair is blonde, red, or gray (IPL does not work without sufficient melanin), your skin is Fitzpatrick VI, you have tattoos in the treatment area, or you prefer faster results (professional laser hair removal is more powerful per session).

### The price question

Let's be honest: Foreo is expensive. A complete set of all four devices exceeds $1,100. The question you should ask yourself is not "which is the best" but "which one do I actually need."

If you are only going to buy one, our recommendation depends on your priority:

- **Cleansing priority:** Luna 4 Plus, but first make sure you are not already achieving the same result with a good manual [Korean double cleanse](/en/hara/k-beauty/double-cleansing-guide)
- **Anti-aging priority:** FAQ 202 (LED phototherapy has the most robust evidence) or Bear 2 if your main concern is sagging
- **Hair removal priority:** Peach 2 if you fit the hair/skin profile, although Braun Silk Expert offers comparable results at a lower price

If your budget is limited and you are looking for a versatile device, the [Medicube AGE-R Booster Pro](/en/hara/k-beauty/medicube-age-r-booster-pro-review) at $159 combines electroporation, EMS, and LED in a single device. It is not as powerful in any individual category as the dedicated Foreo devices, but it offers three technologies for less than half the price of a single Foreo.
