In 2021, a team at Baylor College of Medicine published a study that made waves in the longevity community. It wasn't an exotic compound or a patented molecule. It was two cheap amino acids anyone can buy: glycine and N-acetylcysteine (NAC). The combination, called GlyNAC, reversed multiple aging markers in older adults in just 16 weeks.

<EvidenceBadge level={2} label="Moderate evidence" />

## The Baylor study

**[Kumar et al., 2023 (The Journals of Gerontology)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36210079/):** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 36 older adults (mean age 72). 16 weeks of GlyNAC (glycine 1.33 mmol/kg/day + NAC 0.81 mmol/kg/day) vs placebo.

Results:

- **Intracellular glutathione:** restored to young adult levels (+54%)
- **Oxidative stress:** significant reduction (F2-isoprostanes -28%)
- **Mitochondrial dysfunction:** improved (citrate synthase +30%)
- **Inflammation:** IL-6 significantly reduced
- **Insulin resistance:** HOMA-IR improved
- **Endothelial damage:** reduced
- **Muscle strength:** improved (grip strength +12%)
- **Gait speed:** increased
- **Waist circumference:** reduced (-3 cm)
- **Cognition:** improvement in processing speed and working memory

The effects reversed 12 weeks after stopping GlyNAC. This suggests continuous supplementation is necessary.

### Why it matters

It is one of the few human studies demonstrating simultaneous reversal of multiple hallmarks of aging with a simple intervention. Most longevity supplements improve 1-2 markers. GlyNAC improved 8+.

## Mechanism: glutathione as the cornerstone

### What is glutathione

Glutathione (GSH) is the most abundant antioxidant inside cells. It is synthesized from three amino acids: glycine, cysteine and glutamate. With age, glutathione synthesis declines because glycine and cysteine levels drop (not glutamate).

### Why GlyNAC and not direct glutathione

Oral glutathione is degraded in the digestive tract. It doesn't reach cells intact. GlyNAC provides the two rate-limiting precursors (glycine + cysteine via NAC) so cells synthesize their own glutathione. It is more efficient than taking glutathione directly.

### Cascade of effects

1. GlyNAC restores intracellular glutathione
2. Glutathione normalizes mitochondrial function
3. Healthy mitochondria reduce oxidative stress
4. Lower oxidative stress reduces inflammation
5. Lower inflammation improves insulin sensitivity
6. All of the above improves muscle, cognitive and metabolic function

## Dosing

### Baylor study protocol

- **Glycine:** 100 mg/kg/day (7g for a 70 kg person)
- **NAC:** 100 mg/kg/day (7g for a 70 kg person)
- **Split into 2 doses** (morning and evening)
- **Duration:** 16 weeks minimum

### Simplified practical protocol

- **Glycine:** 3-5g, twice daily (6-10g total)
- **NAC:** 600-900mg, twice daily (1200-1800mg total)
- **Timing:** with or without food (both absorb well)
- **Form:** glycine powder (dissolves in water, slightly sweet), NAC in capsules

## Safety

- **Glycine:** extremely safe. It is a non-essential amino acid the body produces naturally. Doses up to 15g/day used in studies without adverse effects
- **NAC:** safe at doses up to 2400mg/day. Used clinically as an antidote for acetaminophen overdose. May cause mild digestive discomfort initially
- **NAC contraindication:** patients on chemotherapy (consult oncologist). Antioxidants may interfere with oxidative treatments
